# 0006 — Rule hierarchy: invariants, strong defaults, mode-dependent choices

**State:** Open (grammar layer) · **Owner:** Salman Asif · **Date:** 2026-09-11 (v0.9.1) · **Affects:** `docs/rules.md`, `docs/governance.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `SKILL.md`, this trace

**Decision.** Rules are tiered. Eight invariants admit no exception (no invented evidence · Unknown stays Unknown · no colour-only meaning · release ≠ confirmation · provenance where trust depends on it · accessibility · no operating-only content in public builds · one canonical meaning layer). Strong defaults (one hue, weight 500, square, no gradients, minimal shadow, few cards, one signature, a closing line) are the normal expression and may be excepted only with a reasoned, recorded entry in `docs/exceptions/`. Mode-dependent choices (portrait photography in introductions, 600 emphasis in body, a framed module, warmer voice in personal and social) are allowed when they serve the reader. Never excepted: a second accent, colour-only state, bold display type, a decision-free acted-on artifact, fitness costume, a monogram.

**Options considered.** Absolute aesthetic laws (v0.9; superseded because "never" was doing the job of judgment and produced rigid artifacts). No hierarchy, judgment only (rejected: other agents must reproduce the system from `SKILL.md` alone).

**Trade-off.** Three tiers need a place to record exceptions; that is the cost of allowing them at all.

**Evidence and ancestry.** Brief v1.2 §26 (AI ↔ human boundary); `docs/delta-report-v0.9.1.md` §3.

**Risks.** Strong defaults drift into invariants or vice versa through habit; the measurement review (30 days after identity approval or first publication) re-reads the tiers.

**Reopens if.** An exception recurs often enough to be a default, or a default is never excepted and should be an invariant.
