# 0001 — Palette: paper, ink, one ochre

**State:** Locked on identity approval · **Owner:** Salman Asif · **Date:** 2026-09-11 (recorded from Checkpoint 1, 2026-09-10) · **Affects:** every mode; `tokens/palette.css`, `tokens/semantic.css`

**Decision.** Ground paper `#EFE9DF`, surface `#F7F4EE`, ink `#17181A`. One signal hue, ochre `#B8641C`, with `#8C4A12` for text (5.3:1 on paper). Graphite ground `#1D1F22` exists only for transitions, closers and personal mode. No second accent. No red for risk: constraint is ink and a bar.

**Options considered.** A second accent for "risk" or "gate" (rejected: it would let colour carry meaning, breaking invariant 3 and failing greyscale and print). A cool neutral ground (rejected: the warmth that the brief places "in consequence" needs a warm paper, not a cold screen). Pure white ground (rejected: too close to a document default; paper is the recognisable surface).

**Trade-off.** One hue means emphasis is scarce; the tick and the decision are the only ochre objects per artifact. The cost is fewer ways to highlight; the gain is that ochre always means "the decision is here".

**Evidence and ancestry.** Strategic brief v1.2 (north star, emotional signature); Checkpoint 1 direction decision (internal, `strategy/`); contrast measured in `docs/accessibility.md` (ink on paper 14.6:1, ochre text 5.3:1, ink-3 5.0:1).

**Risks.** Ochre on graphite reads differently; graphite is therefore limited to the three uses above. Photography in personal mode must be desaturated toward paper, never ochre-tinted.

**Reopens if.** Identity approval asks for a different hue or a second one; a measured readability failure on a real channel; a channel that cannot render the paper ground.
