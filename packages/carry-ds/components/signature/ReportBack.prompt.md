Report-back card: three fixed sections in fixed order — what came in · what changed · what could not be settled. The third never hides; empty renders "Not yet written".
```jsx
<ReportBack title="Discovery scripts, two weeks on" cameIn="Fourteen replies, nine with a concrete example." changed="I now separate coverage from progression in every review." unsettled="" />
```
Use for Content Brief artifact_type report_back and for any follow-up to a published question. Threshold fn is 'transition' (tick centred).
