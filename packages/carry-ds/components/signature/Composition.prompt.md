The composition family: one visual DNA, seven relationships. Pick the form that matches what the content actually is — do not force a "what people think vs what's true" reveal.
```jsx
<Composition form="claim-support" above="Discovery got faster when we stopped starting from requirements." below={<EvidenceStrip items={[...]}/>} lead="qualify first, scope second, estimate last."/>
<Composition form="outcome-enabled" above="A phased roadmap the client could actually decide on." below="market research → MVP strategy → phases with exit criteria" lead="the next phase gate."/>
<Composition form="intention-reflection" above="Add five kilos." below="Same weight, one more clean rep." lead="the plan gets the weight when the form says so."/>
```
Forms: handle-truth (correct a misleading label/proxy) · claim-support (idea + evidence) · outcome-enabled (portfolio) · question-conditions (open strategy) · direction-consequence (recommendation) · intention-reflection (personal) · observation-development (still forming; usually no lead). `HandleTruth` remains as the specialised first form.
