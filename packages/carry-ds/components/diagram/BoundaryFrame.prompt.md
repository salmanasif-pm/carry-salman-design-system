Scope with dignity: an ink frame around what is owned/committed, a neutral column for what is outside, ochre ports where the two connect.
```jsx
<BoundaryFrame label="Own" meta="signed scope" ports={['weekly sync','escalation']} outside={<>Successor onboarding · future phase structure</>}>
  <LoadNode title="Delivery lead" carries/>
</BoundaryFrame>
```
Never draw an X or red on the outside column — the alternative keeps a legitimate place.
