case study · enterprise healthcare proposal · 2024–25verified wording · client unnamed
the outcome

A complex proposition the client's decision-makers could act on.

how it was enabled

A multi-year enterprise healthcare engagement, contested by international competitors. The problem was not a lack of capability — it was that the proposition was too complex for the people who had to say yes. I led the market research, MVP strategy and solution planning, and shaped a phased roadmap so each phase had a decision attached to it.

Phase the roadmap around the client's decisions, not our delivery plan.

Judgment

What I chose not to do: present the whole platform as one commitment; quote a contract value; claim sole credit. The win was a team outcome and the wording here is the wording I can stand behind.

What was built or changed

Each phase came with a question they could answer, instead of a platform they had to believe in. Phasing trades speed for decidability. If the client had wanted a single commitment, this structure would have slowed us down.
